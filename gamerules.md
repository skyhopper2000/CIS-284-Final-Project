Five‑Card Draw Poker — House Rules (Pot‑Limit Version)
 1. Setup
Use a standard 52‑card deck.

Choose a dealer; the dealer position rotates clockwise each hand.

All players post the ante before cards are dealt.

There are no blinds in this variant.

 2. The Deal
Dealer shuffles and deals five cards face‑down to each player.

Cards are dealt one at a time, starting with the player to the dealer’s left.

 3. First Betting Round (Pot‑Limit)
Action begins with the player to the dealer’s left and proceeds clockwise.

Players may fold, call, or raise.

Pot‑Limit rule:

A player may bet or raise any amount up to the current size of the pot.

The pot size includes all bets made before the action reaches that player.

Betting continues until all players have matched the highest bet or folded.

 4. The Draw
Starting again with the player to the dealer’s left, each active player may discard and replace cards.

Each player may discard 0 to 3 cards.

Dealer gives replacement cards from the top of the deck.

 5. Second Betting Round (Pot‑Limit)
Action begins with the player to the dealer’s left.

Players may check, bet, call, raise, or fold.

Pot‑Limit rule applies again:

Maximum bet or raise is capped at the current pot size at the moment the action reaches the player.

 6. Showdown
If more than one player remains after the final betting round, all players reveal their hands.

Best five‑card poker hand wins the pot.

Standard hand rankings apply.

 7. Next Hand
Winner takes the pot.

Dealer button moves one seat clockwise.

All players ante again, and the next hand begins.
